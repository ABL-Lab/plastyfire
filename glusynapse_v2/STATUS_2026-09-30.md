## What t_drive 3 is (it reads only what the synapse sees)
- **Events.** An event is an upward crossing of 3 mV by the local segment voltage minus its slow baseline (EMA, 300 ms), which is what a bAP looks like at the synapse.
  Events in the 15 ms after the synapse's own pre spike are vetoed, so its own EPSP doesn't count.
- **LTD.** The events feed the eCB trace (S, τ 100 ms; threshold θ_Te; low-pass τ_T). The trace gates presynaptic LTD at the synapse's own pre spikes.
- **Parameters.** No new free ones: θ_V, w and τ_b are fixed.
- **Why voltage and not Ca.** Every amplitude-based local signal (spine/shaft Ca, VDCC, V area) scored ≤ 15% on the Sjöström window test, because the bAP falls about 10× with distance. The event count works at 99% of synapses.
- **Details:** `LOCAL_T_DRIVE.md`.
