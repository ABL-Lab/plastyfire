## Implications for the model

1. **L5→L5 LTD is entirely presynaptic eCB, so ρ-LTD there must be ≈ 0.** Every L5 LTD protocol is abolished by CB1 block: tLTD at 0.1 and 20 Hz (1.01, 1.02), dLTD (≈ 1.00), and Zilberter's L5 10 Hz −10 (1.07). All 68 LTD experiments are presynaptic by CV (Sjöström 2007 Fig 6), and Sjöström 2004 found no postsynaptic component. Postsynaptic BAPTA blocks tLTD but spares ACEA-LTD (Sjöström 2003 Fig 4F), which shows that post Ca is the eCB trigger, not that LTD is expressed postsynaptically. No paper reports BAPTA in Sjöström 2001 or Markram 1997 (checked in both PDFs). So the Markram 10 Hz −10, Sjöström 0.1/10/20 Hz −10, 20 Hz ±25/0, 0.1 Hz −25 and burst −120/−200 rows should all come from the eCB arm. The AM251 arms (#4, #5) bound L5 ρ-LTD to ≲ 5 %.
2. **The underfit is in trigger reliability, not in depth.** The ceiling, 0.71 ± 0.03 (ACEA), equals tLTD (0.73 ± 0.03, n = 60), dLTD (0.68–0.70) and mouse tLTD (0.74–0.78), and agonist plus tLTD occlude (0.77). So d_min = −0.29 is right. Under C-D, the 24-pair mean only reaches 0.72 if every synapse steps. At the v5c fit only 44 % of L5 synapses trigger on a single bAP (ANCHOR_V5C §2), so 0.1 Hz −10 cannot go below about 0.85. The data need P(trigger) ≈ 1 at Δt −10 and −25 for one bAP, the same at 0.1 and 20 Hz (frequency independence: Sjöström 2003 Fig 8B), and P ≈ 0 at −100 to −200 (≈ 1.0–1.05).
3. **The timing window should come from eCB lifetime, not from the Ca decay alone.** Blocking FAAH or AEA uptake extends LTD to −400 ms (≈ 0.68–0.75) without changing −25 ms. A larger post drive gives a longer window: a 5-AP burst gives LTD at −120/−200 (≈ 0.79), and after a 250 ms step, pre spikes 50–400 ms later still give LTD (≈ 0.67). This fits a synapse-local eCB trace: production ∝ post VDCC-Ca integral, decay τ (bracketed by τE1 = 100 ms), read at the own pre spike against a CB1 threshold. Anchors for τ: control −100 gives ≈ 1.0 while −25 gives ≈ 0.65 (Sjöström 2003 Fig 9B, fig values).
4. **The pre gate is glutamate binding at preNMDAR; pre Ca flux is not needed.** APV and ifenprodil block (1.07 / 1.06). In mouse, low-frequency tLTD survives MK-801 (0.78) and 7-CK (0.68) but needs pre GluN1 and pre JNK2 (Thomazeau 2025). Keeping "own pre spike" as the gate is correct. nmdar_block = APV → d = 0 is correct. An MK-801 condition, if ever added, must leave eCB intact. The ACEA arm needs 15–30 Hz pre firing (0.1/5 Hz: 1.00), whereas tLTD works at 0.1 Hz. That means a post-derived signal assists the pre gate at low rates. For the model this is only a reason not to add a pre-frequency term to the eCB arm.
5. **The eCB arm must be off in 50 Hz +10 spike pairing but on in step pairing.** Ifenprodil leaves 50 Hz +10 tLTP unchanged (≈ 1.55 vs ≈ 1.6; target #8 1.59 vs #23 1.57). AM251 raises 200 ms-step LTP by 1/0.76 (Sjöström 2003 and 2007). Sjöström 2001 Model 3 ("LTP pairs annul LTD pairs", RMS 3.8 on random firing) fits the 40/50 Hz −10 LTP (1.51 / 1.70), which shows no LTD component. Candidate mechanism, all synapse-local: an own pre spike that is followed within ≈ +10 ms by a post bAP (an NMDAR-Ca θp event) does not take the eCB step. Anchor ratios: 1.01 (ifen/ctrl at 50 Hz +10) and 1.31 (AM251/ctrl at step). This needs the user's OK as a new mechanism, because v5_DESIGN expects eCB to fire in fast trains.
6. **L2/3→L5 pre-before-post LTD needs postsynaptic ρ-LTD.** In Letzkus 1AP +10 at 1 Hz (0.72, distance-dependent, r = −0.69), the pre spike precedes the only post bAP, so the eCB arm cannot fire. The distal 3AP +10 LTD (0.79) is NMDAR-dependent (APV 1.03) and Ni-insensitive (0.81), which fits moderate NMDAR Ca crossing θd but not θp, as in Chindemi. These rows (#30, #31) anchor a00/a01/γd. Their expression locus is unverified.
7. **The distal 50 Hz rows are probably eCB.** Distal LTD without post spikes is AM251-sensitive (0.71 → 0.99, Sjöström & Häusser 2006). Distal +10 50 Hz pairing gives the same depression (0.80 / 0.70) and was not drug-tested. #38 (0.86) and possibly #36 (3AP −10 proximal 0.89, no drugs) can be eCB. If ρ explains them instead, the model contradicts point 5 at L5.
8. **L2/3→L2/3 LTD is postsynaptic and CB1-independent, so it needs ρ-LTD driven by VDCC Ca.** Zilberter: PPR unchanged, AM251 0.73, APV 0.73, mGluR block 1.02. An intermediate L-VGCC Ca level gives LTD (D890 Ca 0.37 → 0.57; BAPTA 0.05 mM → 0.68), which is a Ca-level ordering θd < c < θp on VDCC Ca. Banerjee 2014 (mouse) also finds postsynaptic expression, but NMDAR-dependent. Two changes follow. (a) θd must be reachable by VDCC Ca alone. (b) If the APV arms are ever used, nmdar_block should remove the NMDAR share of c* rather than freeze ρ. That is against the current user decision, so it is a flag only. The mGluR requirement (1.02) has no synapse-local counterpart in the model. The closest stand-in is gating dep by the own pre spike (glutamate), and it needs a user decision.
9. **This is a uniformity conflict to test, not to fit.** The same single pairing at −10 gives ρ ≈ 1 at L5→L5 (AM251 1.01 ± 0.04) but 0.56 at L2/3→L2/3. The only synapse-local handle is the size of the bAP/VDCC Ca: L2/3 contacts sit 36.5 µm from the soma (Zilberter), while L5→L5 contacts average about 95 µm, with > 80 % within 200 µm (Markram 1997). Compare the per-synapse VDCC-Ca distributions of the two pathways at −10 before adding any term.
10. **Constraints that can be added, all paired:**
    - Sjöström 2004 dLTD 0.69 ± 0.04 (n = 17), with AM251 ≈ 1.00 and ifenprodil ≈ 1.12 (fig) and the three timing points (≈ 1.03 / 0.72 / 0.67). This tests VDCC-Ca eCB without spikes; DLTD_DIAG shows the current trigger fails it.
    - Zilberter L5 10 Hz −10 under AM251, 1.07 ± 0.08 (n = 3), as a mglu_block arm.
    - Sjöström 2001 depolarisation + pre only, 0.645 ± 0.065 (n = 3).
    - Post BAPTA (tLTD ≈ 1.08) adds nothing, because the model gives 1.0 by construction.
    - AA-5-HT / AM404 cannot be represented without an eCB-lifetime parameter. If such a parameter is added (point 3), they become its anchor: −400 ms LTD ≈ 0.68.
    - Not addable as paired targets: Nevian (MCPG flips LTD to 1.53) and Sjöström & Häusser EPSPs-alone (0.71 / AM251 0.99), both extracellular. They remain validation.
