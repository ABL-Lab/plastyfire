Model numbers: CEXP.md (delta-split1, A4 run). Literature found via PubMed (abstracts, plus PMC full text where it was available: Bloodgood 2009, Miyazaki & Ross 2022, Chalifoux & Carter 2010). No PMC full text came back for Koester 1998, Kovalchuk 2000, Yuste 1999, Nevian 2004/2006, Grunditz 2008 or Waters 2003, so for those only the abstract is used. Sabatini 2002 and Holthoff 2002 are abstract-only and give no usable number. No numbers were read off figures.

Caveats: almost all of the quantitative APV and VGCC data come from CA1, juvenile, with exogenous indicator (which favours the slow NMDAR component, Bloodgood 2009). Cortical L5/L2/3 data are qualitative except Koester 1998.

## Implication
- Cpre_APV sits at the resting floor because the model makes almost no non-NMDA spine Ca from one EPSP. Published APV-resistant fractions are 0 to ~30 % (CA1), and spine VGCCs (mainly SNX-482-sensitive CaV2.3) are opened by the local spine-head EPSP. The model is missing that component.
- Rows 6 and 7 (bAP Ca roughly equal to EPSP Ca in L5 basal spines, Koester 1998) point the same way: bAP-driven spine VDCC Ca is too small relative to NMDA Ca. Either the spine VDCC density is too low, or the spine sees only dendritic voltage. With no neck resistance, the spine head never gets the large brief local EPSP that opens VGCCs (Bloodgood 2009, Grunditz 2008). CP-AMPARs are not needed for basal-state synapses (row 4).
- A possible fix: a new spine-head R-type channel under a new name (e.g. `CaR_spine` .mod with a new SUFFIX) in a new emodel or circuit config (e.g. `delta-split1-spCaR`), and/or a spine neck resistance in the synapse. The density would be calibrated independently against two targets: bAP spine Ca about equal to EPSP Ca in L5 basal spines within 80 um (Koester), and an APV-resistant fraction of about 0.1-0.3 (Miyazaki, Bloodgood). It would not be fitted, per the param-anchoring rule.
- Before building it: get the Koester 1998 and Nevian 2006 full texts (PDF) for absolute dCa per bAP and per EPSP in L5 and L2/3 basal spines, and a Mg0 fold value (Sabatini 2002, Kovalchuk 2000 full text). Row 5 is the open gap.
